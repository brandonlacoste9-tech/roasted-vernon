The Legend of Brad

A totally true story.* (*Not true.)

Brad — Founder, CEO, Janitor
The Board of Directors

Legend has it that Brad founded Roasted in the year 20-never, after a dream in which a talking beaver told him Vernon needed "a fake weed store, eh." He woke up, wrote the business plan on a napkin, then ate the napkin. The rest is history. Fictional history.

Today, Brad runs the place alongside his two silent partners — silent because they're cartoons, and cartoons can't talk. Probably for the best. Their entire job is standing on the logo looking cool, and honestly? They're crushing it.

Brad's philosophy is simple: life's short, laugh often, and never trust a grinder you didn't name yourself. He personally taste-tests nothing (it's all imaginary, Brad — put the brownie down), quality-controls everything (by staring at it), and greets every customer like an old friend (there are no customers).

Come as you are. Leave slightly more confused, in the best way. Stay toasty, Vernon.
